---
title: Meta Webhook Setup (Manual Accounts)
description: How to configure your Meta app to forward incoming messages to WhatsRB.
---

Accounts connected via **OAuth (embedded signup)** receive incoming messages automatically — no extra configuration needed.

For **manual accounts** (created with your own credentials), you need to point your Meta app's webhook to WhatsRB.

## Steps

In your [Meta App Dashboard](https://developers.facebook.com/apps) → **WhatsApp → Configuration**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the Callback URL">
    ```
    https://whatsrb.com/webhooks/meta
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Verify Token">
    Enter your WhatsRB API key (`wrb_live_...`) as the verify token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe to webhook fields">
    Enable the **`messages`** field to receive incoming messages and delivery statuses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once configured, Meta will forward all incoming messages and delivery receipts to WhatsRB, which dispatches them to your registered [webhook endpoints](/guides/webhooks).
